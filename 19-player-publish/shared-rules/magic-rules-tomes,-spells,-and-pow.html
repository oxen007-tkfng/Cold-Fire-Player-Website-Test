Summary of Magic Rules – Quick‑Reference Table

# Magic Topic Key Points Details / Mechanics
1 Initial Reading of a Tome May require a reading roll; causes sanity loss (if believer) and Mythos skill gain. • Time: hours‑to‑weeks (Keeper’s discretion).• Sanity loss = roll on the Sanity loss table (if the reader believes in the Mythos).• Gain Cthulhu Mythos (CMI) points as listed in the tome description.
2 Reading Roll Difficulty Depends on the tome’s age/condition. • Printed, < 100 yr, good condition → Regular.• Hand‑written (especially old) → Hard.• Ancient, moldering tomes → Extreme.
3 Full Study of a Tome Must complete an initial reading first; no further roll required. • Study time = Table XI (Mythos Tomes) + any modifiers.• Sanity loss (if believer) as with initial reading.• If tome’s Mythos rating > reader’s Cthulhu Mythos skill → gain full CMI benefit.• If reader’s skill ≥ tome rating → gain only the initial‑reading benefit.• Each subsequent full study doubles the required time.
4 Using a Tome as a Reference Requires a completed full study first. • Spend 1D4 hours consulting the tome.• Make a skill check ≤ tome’s Cthulhu Mythos rating (usually a Knowledge or Mythos roll).
5 Magic Points (MP) – Overspending You can borrow MP from hit points. • For each MP taken, lose 1 HP (or the agreed amount).
6 MP Recovery Regenerates slowly. • 1 MP per hour (more if POW > 100).
7 Learning a Spell from a Mythos Book Starts with an initial reading, then study. • Initial reading (see #1).• Variable study time (typically 2D6 weeks).• Optional Hard INT roll to speed up learning.
8 Learning a Spell from Another Person Faster than from a book. • Same steps as #7, but study time is reduced (Keeper’s discretion).
9 Learning a Spell from a Mythos Entity Very fast acquisition. • Make an INT roll; success = immediate learning (no weeks).
10 Initial Casting of a Spell Requires a Hard POW roll. • Hard POW roll to cast.• If you Push the roll and fail, you may still cast but pay 1D6 × MP plus the same amount of Sanity points, plus any side‑effects the spell specifies.
11 Casting Time & Combat Use Determines when the effect occurs. • Instantaneous: activates on the caster’s DEX + 50 (same round).• Delayed: 2 rounds → activates on the caster’s DEX in the next round; 3 rounds → the following round, etc.
12 Believer vs. Non‑Believer Affects sanity loss and skill gain. • Believers lose sanity when reading tomes and also lose sanity equal to their Cthulhu Mythos skill on first direct Mythos exposure.• Non‑believers gain Cthulhu Mythos skill without sanity loss from reading.
13 Increasing POW (Power) Successful opposed POW rolls can raise POW. • After a successful opposed POW roll (caster beats target), roll 1D100. If the result > current POW or you roll 96‑100, increase POW by 1D10 permanently.• Same rule applies on a Luck 01 roll (critical luck).
14 Special Note – Critical Luck A Luck roll of 01 can boost POW. • Roll 1D100; if result > current POW or you roll 96‑100, increase POW by 1D10 permanently.

Use this table as a cheat‑sheet when handling any magical activity in Call of Cthulhu – from reading forbidden tomes to casting and improving your Power.
